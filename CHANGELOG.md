# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-25)

Built for Kitten Space Agency 2026.11.25.5949. KSAMusic 0.1.0 stays the release for 5707.

- Works on KSA 5949: the atmosphere check and the editor's body list follow the game's new data layout.
- Follows KSA's star-system switch when a save loads: the playlists change with the system, and the launch music state starts fresh.
- A new default soundtrack: Vano's Soundtrack (CC BY-SA 4.0) joins Transfer Windows and Kevin MacLeod. There's now music at launch (Vano's Liftoff) and in the atmosphere (Transfer Windows), Vano's Expeditions and KSA's own tracks in space, and music of its own around the Mun, Minmus, Duna, Eve and Jool. The main menu rotates Vano's Main Theme, KSA's SunSynchronous and Transfer Windows' Home.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-06)

First public release.

- Situational music by scene, situation and body, in Soundtrack Editor Forked's playlist format.
- A KSP-like default soundtrack: Kevin MacLeod's KSP tracks in space, silence on the pad, the ground and in the atmosphere, and Askerad's Transfer Windows for the menu and the buildings.
- Ships Transfer Windows Vol. 1, Vol. 2 and The Dark Sounds Of The Mun EP (Askerad) and Kevin MacLeod's KSP tracks.
- Playlist editor (Music > Edit playlists...): drag and drop, a player, body-specific music picked from the game's own bodies, Reset to preset, applied in game on Save.
- Your own music from KSAMusic/Music in your game profile, and KSA's own tracks from the game install.
