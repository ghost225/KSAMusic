# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-06)

First public release.

- Situational music by scene, situation and body, in Soundtrack Editor Forked's playlist format.
- A KSP-like default soundtrack: Kevin MacLeod's KSP tracks in space, silence on the pad, the ground and in the atmosphere, and Askerad's Transfer Windows for the menu and the buildings.
- Ships Transfer Windows Vol. 1, Vol. 2 and The Dark Sounds Of The Mun EP (Askerad) and Kevin MacLeod's KSP tracks.
- Playlist editor (Music > Edit playlists...): drag and drop, a player, body-specific music picked from the game's own bodies, Reset to preset, applied in game on Save.
- Your own music from KSAMusic/Music in your game profile, and KSA's own tracks from the game install.
